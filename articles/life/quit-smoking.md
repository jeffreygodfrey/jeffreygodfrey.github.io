Today marks the ninth day since I've had a cigarette; so far it is going well, but it is still rough. When i went to visit my doctor last New Years Eve she recommended again that I quit smoking. This time around she offered to send these Commit Lozenges. I got them in the mail about a week or so later. She sent two boxes which have three tubes that contain 24 of the lozenges each for a grand total of 144 lozenges. She sent the mint flavor...

I knew when I got them I would have to at least make an honest attempt at quitting since the doc sent these free of charge. I was not about to just throw away my cigarettes though; that's like throwing money away. Plus this gave me a good way to start at a random time. I've read that you are supposed to do the opposite of what I did, which is set a date to quit and throw away any remaining tobacco products prior to that date. But what I did has so far worked for me, so I'm sticking with it. What I did was start buying single packs until I had smoked all of that pack before I went to sleep. I didn't adjust how much I smoked I just waited until I was out and I had it set in my mind that I would stop smoking the next day and start using these lozenges. Long before I got to this point I had already begun to cut down on how much I smoke quite drastically. A single pack near the end was lasting just under a week; about a year ago I was easily smoking a pack a day.

As I mentioned before the doctor sent the mint flavor of these lozenges. I had the first one on the way to school. The package says it takes about 20-30 minutes to completely dissolve. I don't know what I did wrong that first day, but my drive to school is a little over an hour; I took one as i was driving away from the house and it was just starting to break up as I was pulling into the parking lot at school. This was not the 20-30 minutes as described on the package, so needless to say my breath was very minty for the duration of class. The first thing I noticed after having dissolved the first one is the weird pasty feeling the lozenge leaves in your mouth. This is easily washed away with a drink of some kind of liquid, my choice the first day was soda; the mixture of extreme mint and whatever is in soda makes quite an interesting taste. I was not prepared.

This whole experience has been strange at best, but I'm glad I've quit smoking. Now I look forward to these strange dreams coming to an end soon. Strange as it is, I am smoking in most of these dreams, but that's the most normal thing about them.
